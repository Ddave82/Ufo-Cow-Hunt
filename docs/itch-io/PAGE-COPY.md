# UFO Cow Hunt

**Quiet skies. Questionable intentions.**

Take a low-poly UFO on a very questionable field trip. Beam up cows, camels and polar
bears across three night worlds, recharge on floating energy cores, and dodge the
patrol drones trying to ruin your evening.

## Three worlds. Three timed waves.

- **Farm Night:** moonlit fields, ponds and farm buildings. Your targets: cows.
- **Desert Hunt:** wind-shaped dunes, palm oases and a pyramid. Your targets: camels.
- **Ice Drift:** snowdrifts, frozen lakes and ice peaks. Your targets: polar bears.

Each mission has waves of 10, 15 and 20 animals. Chain collections for combos and
find the hidden human for a bonus. Set drone pressure to Easy, Normal or Hard.

## Play in your browser

Desktop keyboard required. Use **W / arrows** to fly, **A/D / left/right** to turn,
**S / down** to brake, **Space** for the tractor beam, and **Shift** to boost.
**Esc** opens settings; **M** mutes sound. Click the game first to give it keyboard focus.

A modern browser with WebGL2 support is required. Touch controls are not available.
